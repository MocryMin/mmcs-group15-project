# Tier2 — project starting point

Status: extension route and ownership have not yet been agreed in this repository.

Tier2 extends the baseline work. The group needs to choose and document its intended route, such as generation investment or decisions over time under limited information, using the official project brief and clarifications available to its members.

Keep Tier2's assumptions, implementation and results separate from Tier1. When consuming a Tier1 result, record its public path, commit/hash, units and applicable assumptions. A later producer change is a new available input, not an automatic change to this tier's model.

Suggested first shared artifacts:

- A route decision with who agreed, why, and what the extension will demonstrate.
- A pinned dependency on the Tier1 interface or a clearly specified provisional interface.
- A plan for comparing the extension with the baseline under consistent inputs.
- A record of unresolved questions and the evidence needed to settle them.

Cross-tier updates are requests or new public contributions. Ask a Tier1 agent to integrate changes to existing Tier1 files. Keep experiments in member workspaces until deliberately published.
