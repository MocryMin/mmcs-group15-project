# Forecast data contract

The model receives a 24-row tensor with 59 columns for each UTC half-hour decision. The row position aligns forecast fields to a future slot and aligns historical trajectory fields to positions within their own available history. The newest completed and published record for each physical series provides its own history endpoint. See [features.json](features.json) for exact column order, training-only means, standard deviations and coverage.

| Feature source | Availability used in the experiment |
|---|---|
| Elexon AGPT, FUELINST, INDO and ITSDO | Use the most recent edition published by the decision and require the measured interval to have ended. AGPT is the course's 11-fuel background generation scope. FUELINST positive fuel/flow sum gives the five-minute demand signal. Two history blocks cover 48 observations at half-hour spacing, with extra recent five-minute samples. |
| Elexon NDF and WINDFOR | National demand and wind forecasts for each target slot. Choose the latest vintage issued by the decision. WINDFOR uses the corresponding hourly value. |
| NESO daily forecast summaries | Use the mean and count of eligible archived daily forecast points. The summary becomes available once the latest included point has been issued. Calendar fields and recent forecast ages are also available. |
| Nord Pool N2EX day-ahead auction | Use the price and valid flag for each hourly delivery interval. Under the conservative publication convention, each UTC delivery day's auction prices can enter at 00:00 UTC that day. The archived dataset supplies delivery times and auction value, and this convention does not identify individual release vintages. |
| Same-slot history and calendar | Use eligible records 7, 14, 21 and 28 days earlier. Store the available count with each mean. Half-hour slot, weekday and annual seasonality have sine/cosine pairs. |

For physical series the two trajectory columns each contain 24 points, aligned oldest-to-newest, and the more recent block ends at the latest visible record. Availability indicators are explicit input columns. Continuous features with missing values are set to the train mean before standardizing. The public `inference.standardize` function applies the last step after the raw columns have been constructed.

The network and Ridge return standardized residuals with shape (N, 24, 3). Multiply by the target's training standard deviation and add its physical seasonal reference at that slot. Reference paths use the same-slot mean described above, with the latest available source as a fallback where applicable. Price is GBP2020/MWh, while total generation and demand are MWh per half hour. Physical power in GW is 0.002 times the corresponding half-hour energy. Nominal GBP/MWh auction inputs are distinct from real GBP2020 labels derived from the final market index. The course's simulated independent wind farm is included in the generation label, with its V7 daily allocation, and its future realisation is excluded from the inputs.

The two test windows contain 5,833 and 4,393 origins with a complete 12-hour target inside the window. Training is 2022–2024 and validation is January–August 2025. The 2026 date-matched forecasts use the original transform, features and scale. NESO's daily wind summary is missing under the original eligibility rule in the 2026 summer archive, while the separate per-slot wind forecasts cover the full sample.
