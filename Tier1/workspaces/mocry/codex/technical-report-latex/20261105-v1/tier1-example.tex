\section*{Executive summary}
Under economic dispatch in 2023, one 20~MW electrolyser and no battery add
\gbp 8.57 million per year in equivalent value at a hydrogen netback of
\gbp 7.799/kg and a daily offtake limit of 10~t. If electricity sales have
daily priority, the preferred investment is zero. Of the 171.84~GWh used by
the electrolyser under economic dispatch, 13.92~GWh reduces unused generation
and 157.92~GWh displaces direct sales. Investment depends on the buyer's
terms and permission to redirect saleable electricity.

\section{Tier 1 recommendation}
Keep the existing assets while verifying the buyer's netback and daily
acceptance, access to the electricity needed at the site and the priority
of electricity sales. Consider one 20~MW module if the contract clears
the corresponding threshold. Defer battery investment at the assumed
throughput and costs. Revisit it if a paid service or a different operating
window can close the revenue gap.
