For equipment: distinguish installed/nameplate/usable capacity, power/energy, module count, equipment-only/full installation, operating costs, lifetime and replacement. For markets: distinguish possible sale from guaranteed offtake, commodity value from policy support, wholesale series from actual tariffs and physical/network limits from modelling assumptions.

## Conversions and checks

Show the important calculations, especially currencies/price years, MW/MWh, kWh/kg, roundtrip efficiency, usable capacity, annualisation/discounting and per-unit installed costs. State assumptions that make the conversion valid. Attach small reproducible tables/code if useful.
