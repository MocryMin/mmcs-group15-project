- **Site scope.** A 10 t/day hydrogen buyer is the main case. Both policies retain the brief's constructed electricity market and demand data. A 1,559 t/day cap inferred from aggregate UK hydrogen consumption is reported as a separate sensitivity.
- **Tier 1 results.** Table II.3 compares direct sales, electrolysis alone, one four-hour battery block and joint sizing. The fixed-electrolyser value of one four-hour block is £5.63m/year in A and £1.64m/year in B under full historical foresight. The paired two-hour reference in Table II.5 gives zero batteries on the repaired daily data for both policies. With the same five electrolyser modules at half-hour resolution, one block adds £1.05m/year in A and subtracts £1.32m/year in B at the brief's capital-cost basis.
- **Commercial conditions.** The hydrogen price sensitivity includes £9.50/kg and a two-rate case with the support price applied to assumed eligible surplus and a merchant price for the remainder. The report gives the site capital, cash-flow metrics, demand service and terms that would change the decision.
- **Handoff and verification.** The report separates hindsight sizing from forecasts used in dispatch and flags sizing-window overlap. Appendix C explains the respective Xpress and HiGHS checks. Figure II.1 now places the smaller season legend in the clear upper-right corner of the left panel.
