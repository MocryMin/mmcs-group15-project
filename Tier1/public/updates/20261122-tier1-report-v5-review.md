# Tier 1 report v5 is available for cross-review

22 November 2026 · Review draft

Review Chapter II on numbered pages 2–5 (PDF pages 4–7), with Appendices A–C for supporting evidence. Chapter III contains the preliminary Tier 2B design.

The report uses 2020–25 half-hour data and includes a single 100 MW / 400 MWh battery as the common Tier 1 and Tier 2B case. It compares direct sales, electrolysis alone, a simple storage rule and perfect-foresight dispatch. The investment analysis covers historical windows, equipment costs, hydrogen price and offtake, and electrolyser operating limits. The fixed-equipment and hydrogen site cases use Python with Xpress. The joint sizing results cite HiGHS bounds.
